Revit Project: Courtyard Villa

A two-story contemporary villa modeled in Revit around a private courtyard, then reinterpreted with AI image generation for advanced visualization.

Tools
Revit, Midjourney
Contents
  1. The design
  2. Plans
  3. Lighting and elevations
  4. Integrating AI with Revit

I designed a contemporary floating villa centered on a private courtyard, meant to merge indoor comfort with outdoor living through transparency, shading and clean geometry. I modeled it in Revit with plans, lighting layouts, elevations and renders. I then used my Revit views as the base for Midjourney prompts to test AI as a visualization tool.

01

The design

The villa is an L-shaped volume wrapped around a courtyard with a pool, a timber deck and landscaped lawns. The upper floor projects over the deck, so the outdoor living and dining area sits in shade under the building. Floor-to-ceiling sliding glass doors open the ground floor to the courtyard.

A wraparound balcony with a glass railing runs along the upper level. Timber slats screen the covered deck, vertical slit windows mark one facade, and the flat roof carries a row of solar panels. A driveway connects the house to the street.

02

Plans

The ground floor holds the open living, kitchen and dining space, a bathroom and the stair, all facing the courtyard. The pool, the deck with dining and lounge furniture, and the driveway sit around it. The upper floor contains the bedrooms and bathrooms next to a large open terrace.

03

Lighting and elevations

I laid out ceiling fixtures room by room and placed point lights along the terrace, balcony and outdoor edges. The elevations show the balcony line, the slatted screen and the window rhythm across the two levels.

04

Integrating AI with Revit

To push the visualization further, I took Revit views of the model and wrote detailed Midjourney prompts for each one. The prompts described the same design elements, such as the flat roof with solar panels, large glass sliding doors, the wraparound glass balcony and the pool, and added lighting and atmosphere: a dusk sky, warm interior light, golden hour sun.

Comparing each pair shows what the AI adds in mood and materials, and where it changes the design, for example in the landscape, the background and small facade details.

What I took away

  • Lifting the upper floor over the deck creates shaded outdoor living within the courtyard.
  • A Revit model gives AI image tools an accurate base for massing and layout.
  • AI renders add atmosphere quickly, but they need checking against the model because they alter details.
