A small game about sustainable buildings

Net zero, your move

You have 20 coins to spend on a small two-storey building. Choose the strategies that matter most for its climate, and try to make it produce as much energy as it uses.

  • Pick a city. Each climate rewards different moves.
  • Tap a card to add a strategy. Tap again to take it back.
  • Watch the building and the meters change. Reach a rating, then go for net zero.

Missions

  • Make a Boston building net zero
  • Hit Platinum, 80 points or more, in either city

How the numbers work

This is a teaching model, not an energy simulation. Each strategy cuts the building's energy use by a rough percentage that depends on the climate, the way the strategies I study tend to behave in Bangalore's mild, sunny climate and Boston's cold winters. Savings stack on what's left, so each extra measure matters a little less. Solar panels offset energy instead of reducing it. The points loosely follow how LEED rewards energy, water, materials and indoor quality, with Certified at 40, Silver at 50, Gold at 60 and Platinum at 80.
