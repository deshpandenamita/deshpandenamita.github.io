About me

Architect · Building performance · Bangalore and Boston

I'm an architect who fell for the science behind good buildings.

Five years of studio in Bangalore taught me to read a site, study precedents and draw by hand. Graduate school is teaching me to model energy, point a thermal camera at a wall and ask occupants how a building really feels.

I like ideas that are creative and practical at the same time, and spaces that are kind to the people inside them and to the climate outside. Outside of work I make things with my hands: clay, glass mosaic, zentangle, sketches and photographs.
